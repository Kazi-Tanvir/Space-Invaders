- Naming convention: <type>_frame1.png, <type>_frame2.png, <type>_frame3.png (e.g. tank_frame1.png, tank_frame2.png).
- Animation rate: Cycle frames at a consistent interval (e.g. ~0.15–0.25 seconds per frame). The animation should loop seamlessly — no visible pop or stutter between the last and first frame.
